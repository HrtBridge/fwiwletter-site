Voices of Courage Breakfast of Champions

A morning to recognize community champions and support children and families through Voices of Courage Child Advocacy Center.

Get ticketsSponsor this event

A morning with purpose.

Start the morning with people making a difference for children and families. Join us for breakfast, community recognition, and support for Voices of Courage Child Advocacy Center.

Knot-4-Profit Collaborations is organizing the event. Voices of Courage Child Advocacy Center is the benefiting organization, supporting children and families affected by abuse.

Find current ticket and table options through Zeffy. Businesses and individuals interested in sponsorship can explore opportunities through the event’s sponsorship form.
